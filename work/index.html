01 ✦ enterprise voice agent platform ✦ in production

A voice agent that runs at ₹2.29 a minute

Built as backend infrastructure so the client can stand up voice AI customer support for any company that needs it. I costed it layer by layer before writing code, and the stack was chosen against that model rather than against a benchmark.

Speech to text, the language model, speech synthesis and retrieval each carry their own per-minute cost, and each was picked for the cheapest option that still held production quality. It is live and running.

02 ✦ natural language to SQL ✦ facilities SaaS

₹95,000 a month for bad answers

A facilities management platform was paying a vendor copilot to answer questions about their own data badly. I modelled the unit economics layer by layer before writing code: the cheapest model for intent routing, semantic table selection so only relevant schemas reach the language model, and three tiers of cache.

03 ✦ risk intelligence ✦ fintech

The metric was measuring the wrong thing

The reported distress rate was climbing, 21 percent to 27 percent year over year, and it read like a market signal. It was 187 spurious labels from companies that had simply not filed their accounts. I traced it to the label definition rather than to the model, and added a reportability gate that holds the rate inside a point.

Every prediction now carries a SHAP attribution, so an analyst can see which financials drove a flag before acting on it.

result ✦ 92.155% on current-year distress flagging ✦ 187 false labels removed

04 ✦ calling product ✦ diagnostic

I built a diagnostic instead of an architecture

A calling product was hanging up mid-call and the ask was for a better architecture. Before designing one I checked what the recordings could actually tell us.

All nine recordings were 8 kHz mono, so barge-in could not be measured at all. Any architecture chosen on that evidence would have been chosen blind. The finding was that the measurement had to come first.

result ✦ the build was stopped before the money went in
